📖 ONE SOUL LUDO — Rules

  • 🎲 6 par token ghar se bahar.
  • 🔄 6 par extra turn.
  • 🔄 Capture karne par extra turn.
  • 🏠 Token finish/home pahunchne par extra turn.
  • 🎯 Finish ke liye exact number zaroori.
  • 🧱 2 ya zyada same-colour tokens blockade bana sakte hain.
  • 🚫 Opponent blockade enter/pass nahi kiya ja sakta.
  • ⭐ Star/safe square par capture nahi hoga.
  • ❌ 3 consecutive 6 par current turn lose.
  • 🏆 4 tokens finish = winner.
  • 🔁 No legal move + 6 = same player dobara roll; non-6 = next player.
  • 🟢 Move ke baad extra-turn rule ke mutabiq correct next turn.
  • 🔄 Online Firebase sync ke liye Firebase config add ki ja sakti hai.
  • 👥 Room join/leave aur 🚪 leave par seat null.
  • 🆕 New game aur 📋 room-code copy supported.
